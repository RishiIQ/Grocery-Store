01. What Are Cookies & Local Nodes

FreshCart utilizes minor browser-side storage artifacts, including standard HTTP cookies and local storage nodes (`localStorage`), to maintain active user session parameters, shopping bag contents, and theme configurations. These elements allow our network to remember your layout choices seamlessly across page navigations.

02. Categories of Storage Utilized

We categorize our storage nodes into three primary operational brackets:

  • Essential Telemetry Cookies: Required for secure login authentications, shopping bag state persistence, and cryptographic CSRF mitigation.
  • Preference Nodes: Stores your active UI preferences, including dark/light theme choices and LTR/RTL text-direction vectors.
  • Analytical Metrics: Anonymous performance telemetry used to monitor hub loading speeds and detect software anomalies.

03. Managing & Purging Cookies

User Control

You retain full authority to disable or clear browser cookies via your individual client settings. However, please note that purging essential telemetry cookies will terminate your active user session and clear items currently resting in your shopping bag.

04. Updates to Storage Protocols

As our fulfillment infrastructure evolves, this Cookie Policy may experience periodic revisions. Continued utilization of the FreshCart platform following any structural update indicates your formal acknowledgment of the modified storage parameters.
