Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: August 20, 2026 Version 4.2

01. Information We Collect

FreshCart operates on a telemetry-based fulfillment architecture. To provide seamless, 15-minute grocery delivery, we require specific data nodes from our clients. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Identity Data: Full name, verified billing addresses, and cryptographic user IDs.
  • Telemetry Data: Real-time geolocation processing (only active during active delivery dispatches).
  • Transaction Protocol: TLS 256-bit encrypted payment tokens. We do not store raw credit card digits on our native servers.

02. Utilization of Data

Data allocated to the FreshCart network is strictly utilized for fulfillment optimization. We map logistics routes, predict inventory degradation, and secure user accounts against brute-force anomalies. We explicitly guarantee that your dietary preferences and purchasing history are never sold to third-party advertising brokers.

03. Security Architecture

Cryptographic Storage

All user databases operate behind an air-gapped security model with AES-256 encryption at rest. Node transit is secured via strict TLS 1.3 protocols. Our zero-emission courier fleet utilizes rotating cryptographic keys to verify delivery without exposing your permanent residential data to temporary logistical staff.

04. Client Rights & Deletion

Under the Global Data Protection Protocol (GDPP-2025), you retain absolute sovereignty over your profile data. You may execute a complete data purge directly from your secure Customer Dashboard.

Initiating a "Ghost Protocol" via your dashboard will instantly scrub all relational databases of your telemetry, purchase history, and personal identifiers within 72 hours.
